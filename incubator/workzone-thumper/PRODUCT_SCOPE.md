# WorkZone Thumper — Product Scope

## Problem
Roadside utility, survey, paving, tree, emergency-repair, and contractor crews are often exposed on local/non-highway roads where physical rumble strips or major barrier systems are impractical to deploy.

## Product intent
Create a portable system that:
- warns approaching drivers before they reach the crew;
- creates a tactile / in-cabin sensation reminiscent of rumble strips;
- avoids permanent pavement modification;
- can vary cadence or severity based on approach risk;
- remains rapidly deployable by a small crew.

## Initial operating environment
Local/non-highway roads, commonly around 35–45 mph exposure.

## V0
Manual-trigger low-frequency pulse source used to test whether a driver can perceive the warning in-cabin from roughly 75–150 ft without creating unacceptable worker/external exposure.

## V1
Radar-triggered warning using measured speed / closing behavior.

## Later direction
Networked work-zone product family such as Pod / Cone / Zone configurations.
