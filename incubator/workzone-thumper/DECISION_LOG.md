# WorkZone Thumper — Decision Log

## 2026-09-14 — Artificial rumble-strip effect
The product should feel like roadway rumble strips to the driver rather than merely another audible alarm.

## 2026-09-14 — Adaptive cadence
Pulse cadence / severity should change with approach risk, including how far over the target speed the approaching vehicle is and whether it is decelerating.

## 2026-09-14 — Low-frequency directional approach
The product direction uses a directional low-frequency impulse / tactile in-cabin effect rather than relying solely on a conventional high-volume audible warning.

Earlier frequency discussion explored approximately 182–400 Hz as a starting experimental range. This is an experimental design parameter, not a validated specification.

## 2026-09-14 — Prototype before automation
V0 should prove perception and external-exposure behavior manually before integrating radar triggering.

## Naming
Canonical project name: **WorkZone Thumper**.

Historical working names retained for traceability:
- Virtual Rumble Strip
- Active Rumble Zone
