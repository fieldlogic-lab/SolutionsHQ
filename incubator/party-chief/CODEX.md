# Party Chief — Execution Contract

## Mission
Move Party Chief from prototype architecture to validated physical proof, then to a manufacturable commercial product.

## Authority
Read in this order:
1. PRODUCT_SCOPE.md
2. DECISION_LOG.md
3. docs/IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md
4. docs/VALIDATION_PLAN.md
5. docs/COMMERCIALIZATION.md
6. current CAD/BOM/prototype artifacts

## Rules
- Make routine engineering decisions without stopping for confirmation.
- Preserve existing product intent.
- Do not invent production claims that have not been validated.
- Prefer inexpensive reversible prototype decisions before production engineering.
- Record durable product decisions in DECISION_LOG.md.
- Update IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md after meaningful work.
- Keep CAD, BOM, prototype photos, test results, and commercial assumptions organized and traceable.
- Treat the live-keg proof as a hard validation gate.
- Do not confuse prototype-friendly 3D-printed parts with production-approved parts without validation.

## Current Priority
Complete Prototype-to-Proof.
