# Party Chief — Decision Log

## Product identity
Party Chief is the working product name for the mobile draft-beer serving system.

Earlier directions/names should be treated as historical exploration, not parallel products.

## Customer
Primary customer is the beer distributor / keg retailer.

## Business model
Prioritize product sales and distributor-channel validation over operating a rental/event-service business.

## Development philosophy
Prototype to proof before investing in polished production engineering.

## Fabrication
Use 3D printing aggressively for:
- brackets;
- mounts;
- internal organization;
- fixtures;
- cosmetic/prototype parts;
- jigs;
- fit-check components.

Printed components are not assumed production-final unless material, strength, sanitation, heat, and service requirements support them.

## Validation
A live-keg test is a required milestone before production/manufacturing decisions.

## Repository boundary
Party Chief owns:
- product requirements;
- mechanical/electrical/thermal design;
- CAD;
- BOM;
- prototype records;
- validation;
- product economics;
- commercialization documents.

Solutions HQ owns only portfolio status and re-entry metadata once the dedicated repository exists.
