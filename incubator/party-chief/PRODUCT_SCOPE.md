# Party Chief — Product Scope

## Product
A mobile draft-beer serving system intended primarily for beer distributors and keg retailers.

## Core Problem
Keg sales and off-premise serving often lack a clean, professional, mobile draft-serving option that can be transported, set up quickly, kept cold, cleaned, and reused without requiring a full bar installation.

## Product Intent
Create a compact system that:
- accepts a standard keg;
- manages CO2 securely;
- keeps beer at serving temperature;
- presents taps/controls cleanly;
- moves easily;
- can be cleaned and serviced;
- feels like a commercial product rather than improvised party equipment.

## Primary Customer
Beer distributors and keg retailers.

## Secondary Users
Event operators, venues, hospitality operators, clubs, institutions, and other organizations that need mobile draft service.

## Business Direction
The preferred model is product sales through distributors / keg retailers, not building a labor-heavy rental/event-service business.

## Near-Term Definition of Done
Party Chief reaches prototype proof when:
1. a complete physical prototype is assembled;
2. a live keg can be connected and served reliably;
3. temperature, pressure, portability, cleanup, and serviceability are acceptable;
4. at least one distributor/operator can evaluate the unit;
5. major prototype deficiencies are documented;
6. a first-pass BOM and manufacturing assumptions exist.

## Deferred
- production tooling;
- formal certification strategy;
- scaled manufacturing;
- dealer/distributor agreements;
- production packaging;
- broad marketing launch.
