# Party Chief — Commercialization

## Channel Thesis
Sell through beer distributors / keg retailers who already control:
- keg customer relationships;
- order flow;
- delivery routes;
- repeat buyers;
- service conversations.

## Value Proposition
Party Chief can help a distributor sell a more complete draft-beer solution rather than only the keg and temporary hardware.

## Pilot Strategy
1. complete proof prototype;
2. demonstrate with a real keg;
3. place prototype in front of one or more distributor operators;
4. capture operational objections;
5. revise;
6. establish target BOM / wholesale / retail economics;
7. build a small pilot run only after demand signals exist.

## Avoid
Do not prematurely build:
- a large inventory;
- a rental operation;
- a complex service organization;
- expensive tooling;
- polished production marketing before proof.

## Production Questions To Resolve
- production materials;
- sanitation requirements;
- thermal system;
- supplier strategy;
- assembly time;
- service parts;
- shipping dimensions/weight;
- wholesale margin;
- warranty expectations;
- liability/certification considerations.
