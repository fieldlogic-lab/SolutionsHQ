# Party Chief — Prototype Validation Plan

## Goal
Determine whether the prototype is good enough to justify production engineering and distributor pilot work.

## Mechanical
- stable during transport;
- rolls/moves predictably;
- keg retention is secure;
- CO2 cylinder is secured;
- taps and exposed hardware tolerate normal handling;
- enclosure/panels remain serviceable.

## Beverage System
- no leaks;
- stable serving pressure;
- acceptable pour quality;
- reasonable recovery after repeated pours;
- simple connection/disconnection;
- manageable line routing.

## Thermal
- keg remains in acceptable serving-temperature range;
- cooling strategy works under realistic use;
- condensation is controlled;
- no unsafe hot spots or trapped moisture.

## Cleaning
- wetted parts are accessible;
- lines/taps can be flushed;
- spills are recoverable;
- surfaces tolerate routine cleaning.

## Human Factors
- one person can move/setup the system;
- tap operation is obvious;
- service points are accessible;
- distributor staff can understand setup without extensive training.

## Commercial Validation
Capture:
- what distributor staff like;
- what they distrust;
- price reactions;
- transport/storage concerns;
- cleaning/service concerns;
- what would make them buy or refuse the product.

## Proof Milestone
A successful live-keg test plus distributor feedback closes Prototype-to-Proof and opens Production Engineering.
