# AutoDock — Invention Log

## Reconstructed historical record

### 2026-08-30 — Self-centering docking concept
**Source type:** reconstructed.

The core concept was defined as a dock-mounted system that:
- guides a boat into a slip;
- centers it laterally;
- automatically captures/retains it as it moves forward;
- reduces dependence on crew handling lines during approach.

### 2026-08-30 — Passive funnel / guide architecture
**Source type:** reconstructed.

The preferred concept evolved toward:
- passive funnel geometry;
- soft guides/rollers;
- dedicated boat receiver;
- automatic capture/latch;
- counterweighted lines for centering and slack management;
- accommodation for floating docks or vertical tide travel.

### 2026-08-30 — Scale prototype program
**Source type:** reconstructed.

A 1:10 slip and Pursuit-shaped hull prototype was identified as the next development step.

Testing should intentionally include poor approach angles and off-center entries to evaluate:
- self-centering;
- receiver engagement;
- latch reliability;
- release;
- snag/failure modes.

### 2026-09-23 — Formal invention record
**Source type:** contemporaneous Git record.

AutoDock entered the patent-readiness register and received formal version, contributor, and disclosure tracking.
