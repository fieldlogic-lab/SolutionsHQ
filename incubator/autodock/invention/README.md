# AutoDock — Invention Record

This folder preserves the technical evolution of AutoDock for engineering continuity, diligence, and potential future patent work.

It is not a patent filing or legal determination of patentability.

## Focus
Preserve the evolution of:
- passive slip/funnel geometry;
- guide/roller arrangement;
- boat-side receiver geometry;
- automatic capture/latch mechanism;
- counterweighted centering/slack-control arrangement;
- tide / floating-dock accommodation;
- release / fail-safe behavior.

## Rule
Every material prototype geometry or mechanism change gets a dated version entry.
