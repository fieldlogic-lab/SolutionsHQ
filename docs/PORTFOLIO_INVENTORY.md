## Do not include in Solutions HQ portfolio
These may be important work, but they are not Massi Solutions product projects:
- National Grid programs and internal reorganization work.
- TEDx talk preparation.
- Sayville home move / renovation planning.
- Personal boat maintenance or one-off upgrades unless they become a commercial product.
- Personal finance planning.
- Family logistics.
- One-off research, purchase decisions, or writing tasks.

## Promotion rule
A project graduates from Innovation Lab into the main Solutions HQ listing when it has:
1. a distinct purpose and user/customer;
2. independent decisions or architecture;
3. more than one meaningful work session or a committed build/validation path;
4. a durable next step;
5. enough scope that losing its history would materially slow re-entry.

Once promoted, it should have its own repository or other explicit source-of-truth location and a project scope document.
