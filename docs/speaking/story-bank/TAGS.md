# Story Bank Tag Taxonomy

Tags should describe the **idea demonstrated by the story**, not merely the setting.

## Core Tags

### commitment
A person chooses to care, engage, improve, or take ownership beyond minimum compliance.

### compliance
A person follows an instruction or rule without necessarily believing in it or taking ownership.

### alignment
People, incentives, actions, timing, or systems point in the same direction.

### control
Performance is being pursued primarily through direction, restriction, authority, or supervision.

### ownership
A person treats the outcome as theirs to influence rather than someone else's instruction to execute.

### trust
Performance depends on confidence in another person, process, team, or system.

### standards
Clear non-negotiable expectations, especially where commitment remains voluntary.

### accountability
People can see whether commitments, standards, or outcomes were actually met.

### feedback
A system makes performance visible quickly enough to influence behavior.

### friction
Avoidable effort, confusion, steps, delay, or resistance that makes good behavior harder.

### strategic-laziness
A refusal to spend effort where design, automation, simplification, or leverage can produce the same or better result.

### system-design
The environment, workflow, incentives, information, tools, or rules are deliberately shaped to produce better outcomes.

### leadership
Someone changes the quality of decisions, alignment, confidence, or performance of others.

### team-dynamics
Performance depends on interactions between people, not just individual capability.

### discretionary-effort
The difference between doing what is required and voluntarily contributing judgment, creativity, energy, or care.

### decision-making
The story reveals how people choose under uncertainty, pressure, competing priorities, or incomplete information.

### risk
The system must balance performance with safety, consequence, or uncertainty.

### communication
Information quality, timing, clarity, or shared language materially affects performance.

### individual-vs-system
The story exposes a mismatch between individual talent/effort and the performance of the larger system.

## Tagging Rules

1. Choose **one primary tag**.
2. Use no more than **five secondary tags** unless genuinely necessary.
3. Prefer the idea the story **proves**, not the idea merely mentioned.
4. Do not create a new tag for a one-off concept unless the same idea appears in multiple stories.
5. A story can change tags as its interpretation develops.
6. Domain and audience are separate metadata and should not be used as conceptual tags.

## Platform Test

A strong bank should eventually contain multiple high-quality stories for each of these core territories:

- Commitment vs. compliance
- Alignment vs. control
- People problem vs. system problem
- Friction and strategic laziness
- Feedback and truth
- Individual excellence vs. collective performance
